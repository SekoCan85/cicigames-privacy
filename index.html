Draft — not the final release policy. Prepared on October 2, 2026. Advertising, children's privacy, and consent settings must be verified before this policy is used for the published game.

1. About this policy

This policy describes information handling in Crazy Tetris, an Android puzzle game developed under the name CiciGames. For privacy questions, contact serkancaniklioglu@yahoo.com.

2. Game data stored on your device

The game stores information such as scores, records, and sound preferences locally on your device to support gameplay. The current game does not offer player accounts or an online leaderboard.

You can remove local game data through Android's app storage settings. Doing so also resets the saved game information. Device backup and restoration may depend on your Android settings.

3. Rewarded advertising

Crazy Tetris uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Completing an eligible advertisement grants an in-game reward.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. Depending on configuration and applicable privacy settings, this can include IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers.

Advertising identifier collection and ad personalization depend on the final implementation and applicable user choices. Restrictions on personalized advertising do not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or removed. Clearing game data does not delete information already processed by advertising providers.

Google's retention practices are described in its privacy policy. For information held by CiciGames through support correspondence, you can request access or deletion using the contact address above.
