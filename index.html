CiciGames · Crazy Tetris

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy describes how information is handled in Crazy Tetris, an Android puzzle game developed under the name CiciGames. For privacy questions, contact serkancaniklioglu@yahoo.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Crazy Tetris stores gameplay information, such as scores and records, and settings, such as sound preferences, on your device. It also saves your selected age group on your device to configure the game experience. The game does not ask for your exact date of birth.

Crazy Tetris does not currently offer player accounts or an online leaderboard. You can remove locally stored game information through Android's app storage settings. Doing so may reset your progress, settings, and age-group selection. Device backup and restoration depend on your Android settings.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

Crazy Tetris uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements. You choose whether to watch an available advertisement. Completing an eligible advertisement grants an in-game continue.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information for advertising, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. Depending on the player's age group, applicable restrictions, and privacy choices, this information can include IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, advertisement and app interactions, diagnostic information, and permitted device identifiers.

Restrictions on personalized advertising do not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing. For information about Google's practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

4. Age groups and privacy choices

The game is intended for both children and adults. When you first open the game, it asks you to select an age group: under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over. The selected group is saved on your device. The game does not request an advertisement before an age group is selected.

The game applies restricted advertising settings to all selected groups under 18 and limits the requested advertisement content rating to G. The age-group selection is used to configure advertising and privacy controls; it is not an identity check.

The game checks whether a Google privacy message is required before requesting advertisements. Where required, Google presents privacy choices. If Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, a "Privacy Options" button is shown in the game so that you can revisit those choices. If an advertisement cannot be requested, you can still play the game.

Parents or guardians may contact CiciGames with questions or concerns about a child's information. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information in your message.

5. Support messages

If you email CiciGames, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Messages are processed through our email provider.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored game information remains on your device until it is cleared or removed, subject to your device's backup settings. Clearing game data does not delete information already processed by Google.

Google's retention practices are described in its privacy policy. CiciGames keeps support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and deletes it within 12 months of the last correspondence, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request access to or deletion of support correspondence by emailing the privacy contact below.

7. Security

Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypts collected data in transit using TLS. No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game's services or data practices change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when a revised policy takes effect.
